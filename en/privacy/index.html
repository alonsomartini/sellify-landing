Privacy

What data sellify.us collects, what we use it for, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Sellify Agency, operated by Cortafuegos LLC. For anything about privacy, write to alonso@sellify-agency.com.

What we collect and why

  • What you type into the contact form: name, email, brand, where you sell, and monthly sales. The site does not store that data. It opens a WhatsApp message with it, and you decide whether to send it.
  • Your booking details, if you book an audit. Bookings run on Google Calendar, which passes us your name, your email, and the time you picked.
  • Visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages they view. It uses no cookies and does not follow you across sites or from one day to the next.
  • A language cookie. If you switch languages, we store a cookie called lang for one year to remember your choice. It cannot identify you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. It is only handled by the providers that run the site: Vercel (hosting and statistics), Google (booking), WhatsApp (messages), and Amazon (some product photos). These providers may process data in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your email and your messages while we are in conversation with you, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete your data, or to stop writing to you, by emailing alonso@sellify-agency.com. We reply within 30 days. If you are in the European Union and believe we did not handle your request, you can file a complaint with your country's data protection authority.
