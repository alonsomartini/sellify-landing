Selling more shouldn't mean making less.

If you can't tell which campaign or product is making you money, every month on Amazon is a gamble. Here's what we find most often when we audit an account:

  • You're paying for clicks that don't sell.

    Search terms with clicks and no orders that nobody has reviewed in months.

  • Your ACoS looks fine. Your margin doesn't.

    Leave out fees, returns, and product cost, and your advertising can be losing money.

  • Your products don't show up where people buy.

    Main keywords missing from the title or not indexed on the listing.

  • You run out of stock, or sit on too much.

    A stockout costs you rank. Overstock costs you long-term storage fees.
